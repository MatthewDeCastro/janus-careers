BMS Delivery Engineer

United Kingdom (London) · On-site · Full-time · Apply by 23 November 2026

As a Digitalisation Delivery Engineer, you're the engineer who turns physical buildings into the structured, high-fidelity data models our agentic AI operates on, and increasingly, the engineer who trains and certifies the client and partner teams who take over platform operation after you. Working hands-on with Project Managers and BIM Modellers, you'll own delivery of both static building data and dynamic IoT data on live projects, then hand off your expertise through structured training and certification. Your work determines whether a building can run autonomously, and whether the partners who take it from there are equipped to keep it that way.

About Janus Digital

Janus Digital™ is the agentic AI platform that runs the world's real assets. It is built on 15+ years of foundational R&D, 76 granted patents and over $100M invested, and is live across 10,000+ building datasets in 253 cities and 10+ asset classes, offices, hotels, shopping centres, hospitals, data centres and more. We work on an outcome-based model: we earn on the verified value the platform creates.

What you will do

  • Own end-to-end delivery of building data models: as-built drawing collection, BIM verification against physical sites, spatial and MEP equipment registration, relationship mapping, QR code tagging and final client acceptance.
  • Execute the full IoT delivery workflow: point list encoding, binding table preparation, gateway configuration and protocol commissioning across BACnet, Modbus, MQTT, OPC UA and KNX.
  • Bind IoT data points to their building models, verify data accuracy and connectivity, and commission complete subsystems: energy metering, lighting, chiller/AHU controls and environmental monitoring, end to end.
  • Train and certify client and partner engineers, including property managers, hotel operators and shopping centre administrators, to configure, operate and maintain the platform independently after handover, through a mix of virtual, in-person and hands-on lab sessions.
  • Own recertification for every partner team you've trained, running annual refreshers to keep operational and data-quality standards consistent across sites.
  • Meet global quality benchmarks (data precision ≥95%, completeness ≥98%, IoT connectivity ≥95%, accuracy ≥98%) through rigorous self- and peer-review before client submission.
  • Collaborate daily with Project Managers, BIM Modellers and Solution Architects on delivery planning, model accuracy and platform configuration.
  • Diagnose and resolve field issues, including BIM-to-reality discrepancies, multi-protocol IoT integration problems and data-modelling edge cases, escalating complex cases to Tier-3 support.

What you will bring

  • 2+ years' hands-on experience in building MEP design, construction, building digitalisation, BIM delivery, Building Management Systems, IoT deployment, or smart building technology.
  • Solid understanding of building MEP systems (HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, security, building automation/DDC), with the ability to read mechanical/electrical drawings and identify equipment on site.
  • Working proficiency with BIM tools (Revit) and CAD/IFC drawing interpretation.
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to maintain consistent data-quality standards across large data volumes.
  • Understanding of IoT communication protocols (BACnet, Modbus, MQTT, OPC UA) and practical experience with IoT deployment or device commissioning.
  • Strong collaboration and communication skills in cross-functional, multicultural project teams.
  • Professional fluency in English.
  • Right to work in the UK.
  • Diploma or Bachelor's degree in Engineering (Electrical, Mechanical, Building Services), Facilities Management, Information Technology, or a related field.

Nice to have

  • Experience designing or delivering technical training or certification programmes to external or non-technical audiences.
  • Coaching, presentation and training-delivery skills, comfortable teaching technical content clearly to partner audiences, including those who don't have English as a first language.
  • Working knowledge of UK building standards and CIBSE guidance (Part L, Part F, TM44).
  • Wire-level IoT protocol debugging experience (BACnetScan, Modbus Master, UAExpert, MqttInsight or similar).
  • Facility management or building operations experience.
  • Strong structured-data thinking: translating complex MEP hierarchies into clear, machine-readable classifications.
  • Prior field delivery experience on construction sites or in commissioning environments.
