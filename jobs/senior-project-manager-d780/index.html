Senior Project Manager

  • United Kingdom (London)
  • On-site
  • Full-time
  • Apply by 11 November 2026

As Senior Project Manager, you're the first responsible person for turning our AI vision into a physical reality, not just managing a timeline but orchestrating the transformation of a building into a fully autonomous, AI-governed asset. Based in London, you'll own a project's financial performance, technical scope, regulatory compliance and risk profile from initiation through to full autonomous operation, bridging the deterministic world of mechanical and electrical engineering with the probabilistic world of generative AI.

About Janus Digital

Janus Digital™ is the agentic AI platform that runs the world's real assets. It is built on 15+ years of foundational R&D, 76 granted patents and over $100M invested, and is live across 10,000+ building datasets in 253 cities and 10+ asset classes, offices, hotels, shopping centres, hospitals, data centres and more. We work on an outcome-based model: we earn on the verified value the platform creates.

What you will do

  • Own the full project lifecycle, initiation through closing, with total accountability for P&L, margin and revenue realisation.
  • Define the Work Breakdown Structure for complex building automation and legacy-system integration projects, and manage delivery across multiple concurrent sites.
  • Oversee installation of IoT devices, gateways and BMS integrations, coordinating MEP reviews to identify infrastructure limitations before deployment.
  • Manage the transition from supervised AI operation to reliable autonomy, working with AgentOps engineers on telemetry, monitoring and escalation design.
  • Identify and mitigate cyber-physical risk, and manage project change requests against budget, timeline and compliance.
  • Ensure delivery meets the UK regulatory landscape (BREEAM, the Building Safety Act 2022, ISO 19650, UK GDPR) in partnership with specialist teams.
  • Coordinate Solution Architects, Delivery Engineers and AI/ML teams to unify physical, digital and regulatory workstreams.
  • Train the trainer: Project manager should have experience in training people. Creating training programmes and delivering training.

What you will bring

  • 8+ years of experience within a Building Management Systems, MEP or construction/technology delivery environment.
  • Project Management certification, PMP, PRINCE2 or equivalent.
  • Exceptional leadership, negotiation and stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to stay decisive under high-pressure, on-site conditions.
  • Fluent written and spoken English.
  • Right to work in the UK.

Nice to have

  • Proven, demonstrable ownership of project P&L: costing, risk and profit-and-loss accountability.
  • Agile/Scrum delivery experience (PMI-ACP or CSM).
  • Technical literacy in structured digital building models, BIM, ISO 19650 and smart building protocols (BACnet, Modbus, KNX).
  • Working knowledge of converged IT/OT security and data-privacy frameworks (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).
  • Experience within the UK building or construction sector.
  • Chartered status or membership of a relevant professional body (CIBSE, IET, RICS or APM).
